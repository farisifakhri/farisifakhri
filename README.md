## 👋 About Me

I'm an **Informatics Engineering student at UIN Syarif Hidayatullah Jakarta** interested in computer vision, artificial intelligence, full-stack development, and information systems.

I enjoy turning real-world challenges into practical digital solutions — from AI-assisted futsal video analysis to institutional workflow automation and community technology.

Beyond software engineering, I am a **licensed Level 2 futsal referee in Banten** and contribute to youth leadership and community initiatives. These experiences shape my approach to technology: think critically, work systematically, and make decisions based on evidence.

- 🔭 Building AI-assisted sports analysis and workflow-based information systems.
- 🤖 Exploring object detection, multi-object tracking, homography, RAG, and knowledge graphs.
- 💻 Developing web applications with a focus on usability, maintainability, and real operational needs.
- 🕌 Contributing to digital dakwah and community-based technology initiatives.
- ⚽ Connecting sports officiating experience with computer vision research.
